A show that has ended will never get a new episode, so there is nothing to
monitor — and if nobody has watched it in a year, little reason to keep it.
Sonarr knows the first fact; Librariarr's play history knows the second.

## Before you start

- Sonarr connected under **Settings → Integrations**. The **Ended** and
  **Has Unaired** fields read from it.
- Play history established on the targeted servers, from the server's own
  history or from [Tracearr](/docs/integrations/tracearr/); see the
  [delete unwatched media](/docs/guides/delete-unwatched-media/#before-you-start)
  guide.

## Step one: stop monitoring

Start with the non-destructive version. On the **Series** tab with
**Match against entire series** on, the top group holds:

- **Ended** (`arrEnded`) **equals** `true` — Sonarr reports the show as ended.
- **Has Unaired** (`arrHasUnaired`) **equals** `false` — nothing is still due to air (a
  show marked ended can have a final episode pending).

and a nested group (**Add Group**) joined with **OR** inside:

- **Watched Episode %** (`watchedEpisodePercentage`) **equals** `0` — nobody has ever started it.
- **Series Last Played** (`seriesLastPlayedAt`) **More Than X Days Ago** `365` — the newest play
  across every episode is over a year old. A show nobody has played has no
  play date at all, so this condition alone would skip it.

Action **Unmonitor Only**, delay of a few days. Sonarr stops searching for
upgrades; every file stays. Import it from the
[Rule Set Library](/docs/guides/rule-set-library/#ended-shows-nobody-watches).

**Series Last Played** is what makes this safe for a show someone is
working through: it is the most recent play of *any* episode, so an old
season watched last year does not count against a show whose newest
episode was played last week.

## Step two: remove them

Once you trust the matches, a second rule set can delete. Use the same
conditions plus **Watched Episode %** (`watchedEpisodePercentage`) **less than** `25` — shows the
household abandoned early rather than finished — with **Delete from Sonarr**
and a delay of two weeks. **Delete from Sonarr** removes the whole series
and its files; it is refused outright when *any* episode of the show is on
the Exceptions list, even one the rule never matched.

To keep what people did finish, invert the test: **Watched Episode %** (`watchedEpisodePercentage`)
**greater than** `90` with **Unmonitor Only** stops Sonarr spending
bandwidth on upgrades for shows that have already been watched through.

<Aside type="tip">
  Attach a [Leaving Soon collection](/docs/guides/leaving-soon-collection/) to
  the delete rule set so the shows appear on the Plex home screen for the
  two weeks before they go.
</Aside>

## Related fields

- **Series Type** (`arrSeriesType`) — Sonarr's series type (standard, daily, anime), to keep
  anime out of a rule written for standard ordering.
- **Available Episode Count** (`availableEpisodeCount`) — how many episodes are actually on disk, so a
  show you only ever had two episodes of can be treated differently from a
  complete run.
- **Last Episode Added** (`lastEpisodeAddedAt`) — when the newest episode arrived; `more than X
  days ago` keeps a show that is still being filled in off the list.
